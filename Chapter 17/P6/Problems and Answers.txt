Based on i've observed about -P(1, 10, 25, 50, 75, 90, 99), it seems that the smaller the percent allocated, the smaller size of data being requested often and it's more often freeing data immediately while the more larger the percent allocated, the more larger data requested often and less often the blocks being freed that it will reach to the point where it's not being freed, just like -P 99.
